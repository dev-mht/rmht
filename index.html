Knowledge Base

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

How does the $500K Milestone system work?
Every time the Market Cap grows by $500,000 and holds, 1.5% of the remaining vault is released. Holding matters: the contract reads a 30-minute time-weighted average price, then requires three confirmations ten minutes apart inside a two-hour window. Any drop back below the milestone resets the count. The release is triggered by pokeMilestone(), a public function anyone can call — there is no keeper, no bot and no subscription to fund, so no third party can ever stall the mechanism. 100% goes to eligible holders pro-rata — no marketing split, no auto-liquidity split.
What happens if the Market Cap drops after a payout?
Once a Milestone is reached and the reward distributed, that level is permanently validated. A price drop does not cancel or reverse the reward. The next trigger requires a new +$500K MCap from the last validated milestone.
Is the Milestone Vault secure?
Yes. The Vault is governed entirely by the Smart Contract, scanned by SolidityScan, Slither, Mythril, Wake, Aderyn, Solhint, Surya and 311 Foundry tests — no Critical, High or Medium item left open. Review status. Releases can only happen through the milestone state machine — three confirmations, ten minutes apart, on a 30-minute time-weighted average price. Ownership will be renounced on all four contracts right after launch. One role is deliberately kept and survives that renouncement: feedGovernor, which can replace the address of a Chainlink oracle and nothing else — it cannot touch the pool, the allocations, the vault or any balance. It exists because on $MHT V2 an oracle failed after ownership had been renounced, with no way left to repair it. It is held by a hardware wallet and can be destroyed permanently once the feeds are settled. Full detail on the protocol page.
How do I receive my rewards?
Rewards accrue pro-rata based on your RMHT balance, tracked continuously since the last claim. Only eligible supply counts (excludes vault, burn address, and the Uniswap pool). Use the Claim button above to collect — RMHT uses a claim model, not an automatic push to your wallet.
How is the vault release split?
Each release is split automatically by the contract: 100% goes directly to eligible holders pro-rata. There is no marketing split, unlike $MHT on BSC — this is one of the improvements built into $RMHT.
Is there a Buy/Sell tax on $RMHT?
No. Unlike $MHT on BSC, $RMHT has 0% buy/sell tax. Liquidity fees are captured separately, on the Uniswap v3 position itself, through a dedicated non-withdrawable custodian contract — not through a token tax.
